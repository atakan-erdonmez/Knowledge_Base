**Note:** While not a standalone dashboard service, the **AWS Cost and Usage Report (CUR)** is the absolute single source of truth for all billing data. It automatically delivers the most granular, itemized breakdown of your AWS costs (down to the hourly/resource level) into an Amazon S3 bucket, serving as the foundational data source for advanced data queries or third-party FinOps tools.
