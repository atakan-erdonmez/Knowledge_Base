AWS Budgets allows you to plan and manage your cloud expenses proactively by setting custom financial or usage limits. You can configure it to track your actual or forecasted costs, usage, and even Reserved Instance (RI) or Savings Plans utilization, automatically triggering alerts via email or SMS when predefined thresholds are breached. It also supports automated Budget Actions to temporarily restrict resource provisioning or enforce service control policies when a budget is exceeded.
