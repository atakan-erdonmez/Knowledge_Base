AWS Trusted Advisor is a guidance tool that **analyzes your AWS environment** and **provides recommendations** to help you follow AWS best practices. It examines your account across multiple dimensions—**including cost optimization, performance, security, fault tolerance, and service limits—and flags areas where improvements can be made**. Think of it as an automated consultant that continuously scans your infrastructure to identify potential issues.

The service provides **real-time insights with a dashboard** that shows your compliance status across different categories. Some checks run continuously at no cost (like service limit monitoring), while others are available with an AWS Support plan subscription. Trusted Advisor recommendations might include things like removing unused resources, adjusting instance types for better efficiency, closing security group ports unnecessarily exposed to the internet, or enabling redundancy for critical services.

By regularly reviewing Trusted Advisor findings and acting on its recommendations, organizations can reduce unnecessary spending, improve system reliability, enhance security posture, and avoid hitting AWS service quotas that could disrupt operations. It's particularly valuable for teams that want automated guidance without hiring dedicated AWS architects to manually audit their infrastructure.
